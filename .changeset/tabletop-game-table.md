Add game tables (`tabletop-table`) and seats (`tabletop-seat`). A table is rectangular, round or a regular polygon (3–8 sides); what is put on it becomes its child and moves with it. Seats are shapes of their own with a label; put on a table they snap onto its outline, facing it, and `seatViewRect` gives the camera rect that shows the whole table from that seat. New actions add a table (with two players), add a player, spread players evenly, and set the table's form, camera and a player's name.
