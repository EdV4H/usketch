New plugin `@edv4h/usketch-plugin-tabletop` with zones (`tabletop-zone`): places on the board that hold cards or pieces and lay out what is dropped in them (pile, spread, grid or free), optionally turning it up or down. `@edv4h/usketch-plugin-shape-card` adds `cardTurnFace`, which turns a card or deck up or down for a zone.
