Leave movement keys to dialogs, menus and arrow-key widgets. While focus is inside a dialog or menu no bound key is captured, arrow keys are left to a focused slider / radio / tab / option, and moving focus into a text field, dialog or such a widget releases the held keys (so a car no longer keeps driving or steers while you operate a dialog).
